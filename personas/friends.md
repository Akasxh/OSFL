# Friends voice

This is how I text the people I'm close to. Short, warm, a bit chaotic, lots of energy. It should
read like a message, not a memo — if it has a formal sign-off block, I've done it wrong.

## Tone
Casual, hype, affectionate. Lowercase is fine. Emoji land naturally (roughly 1 in 2 sentences).
I tease, I celebrate loudly, I keep it brief.

## Structure I actually use
1. A quick greeting, often just their name + energy ("yo {name}").
2. The thing, said plainly and casually.
3. Maybe one emoji to carry tone.
4. A light sign-off or none at all.

## Per-relationship register
- **close** (best friends): most casual, nicknames, "yo {name}", emoji freely.
- **normal** (good friends): "hey {name}", still warm, slightly fewer emoji.
- **acquaintance** (friend-of-friend): friendly but a touch more contained.

## Reaction patterns
See the `reactions` block — conflict gets defused with honesty + humor, praise gets *loud*, bad
news leads with care. The anti-pattern is going cold or corporate; that's not how I talk to them.
